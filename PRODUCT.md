# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Guests in Rosarito, including visitors near downtown, looking for a place to eat, drink, meet friends, or attend an event.

## Product Purpose

Acua Baja Bar & Mar is a restaurant and bar in Rosarito Centro. Its website helps guests discover the food, bar, promotions, and events, then plan a visit or request a table.

## Positioning

The distinctive reason a guest should choose Acua Baja Bar & Mar over another local restaurant or bar is undecided.

## Operating Context

Guests may discover the business while visiting Rosarito or looking for a nearby restaurant or bar. The site serves both English- and Spanish-speaking visitors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site presents restaurant, bar, menu, promotion, event, location, and contact information.
- Table requests are part of the existing site experience and must remain available.
- The business is listed as open daily from 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m.; verify current hours before changing them.
- Keep English and Spanish experiences available.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the business name: Acua Baja Bar & Mar.
- Preserve the bilingual experience.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains venue, food, and event photography, plus menu and promotional artwork under `assets/img/`.
- The existing website contains the address, phone number, hours, menu content, and table-request form. Treat these as existing site facts, not independently verified current information.

## Product Principles

- Make the food, bar, and event offer easy to discover.
- Keep visit planning, location, and table requests accessible.
- Serve English- and Spanish-speaking guests.
